## Numbers are transcribed as such

Numbers are transcribed as they appear in the document, whether in Roman or Arabic characters. 
In the former case, ramist standardization (u, v, i, j) must be applied. In the case of numbers written with superscript letters,
they will be represented by superscript numbers too. We recommend systematically framing them with dots, as is frequently 
done in manuscripts. The groups of numbers should be clearly separated. 
The punctuation surrounding the figures should be retained where it exists.
