In **modern sources**, punctuation signs are not modernized (the [diastoles](./segmentation.md) are kept), but, when used in the source, modern punctuation signs (like the comma ",") should be transcribed as such. In more recent documents, we use modern punctuation signs.

Spacing should be normalized before and after punctuation such as there should always be a space after a punctuation sign (except "(", "[" or "⟦", etc.) but there should not be a space before a punctuation sign. 

When several punctuation signs are used to fill a blank between two lines (as in "name: ........Jane...."), it is acceptable to not try to type as many characters as traced, but to limit ourselves to 4 repetitions of the same symbol. We recommend using dashes ("-" [U+002D]) rather than underscores or other types of dashes.
